## Introducing Amalisidi — Effortless Archive management for receipts and invoices

Managing receipts and invoices shouldn’t be a chore. Whether you’re an individual tracking rent and groceries or a small business juggling client invoices, keeping things organized can quickly get messy.

That’s why I built Amalisidi, a word that means receipts in isiZulu — a simple, powerful platform that helps you store, organize, and automate your receipts and invoices in one secure place.

## Why I Built It

Over the years, I've gathered a bunch of receipts and invoices and placed them in folder, but found it very tedious to organize them, I've often needed to reference a receipt for warranty claims or checked how the price of an item has increased over time, existing solutions were either to complicated or did not suite my needs.

I wanted something that:

* Keeps everything organized into categories.

* Lets me schedule recurring receipts (like rent or subscriptions).

* Works everywhere — web, mobile, even command line.

* Plays nice with my existing tools and workflows.

* Allow me to immediately capture details about a purchase on mobile.

So I built it — for myself first, and now, for everyone who wants peace of mind when it comes to archiving there receipts and invoices.
It has also helped tremendously during the tax period, allowing me to select a date range and obtaining all the relevant documents as a zip file.


## What You Can Do with Amalisidi

Here’s what makes it special:

* Archive your receipts and invoices — upload, and categorize them with ease.

* Create recurring schedules — set up automatic entries for regular payments like rent or subscriptions.

* Import from CSV, export to ZIP — bring your data in or out anytime.

* Access anywhere — web, mobile, or even via our command-line interface.

* Connect via API — integrate with your own systems or automation tools.

* Simple dashboard — get insights without the clutter.


## Who It's For

* Individuals who want a better way to archive expenses.

* Freelancers managing clients and invoices.

* Small businesses that need a light archiving tool without the heavy setup.
